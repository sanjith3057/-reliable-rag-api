# Orbit B2 Rugged Scanner: Product Specification

The Orbit B2 is Lumenfold's rugged handheld inventory scanner for cold storage, yards and large distribution centres.

## Power

The Orbit B2 has a battery life of 22 hours of continuous scanning from its 6,800 mAh battery. A full charge of the Orbit B2 takes 3 hours over USB-C. The Orbit B2 battery is hot-swappable, so it can be replaced without powering the device off.

## Physical

The Orbit B2 weighs 420 grams. The Orbit B2 has an IP68 rating, which protects against dust and continuous immersion in water. Its display is a 5.5 inch touchscreen that works with gloves. The Orbit B2 operates between minus 20 and 60 degrees Celsius.

## Scanning

The Orbit B2 reads 1D and 2D barcodes at a range of up to 15 metres. It scans up to 90 codes per second.

## Storage and connectivity

The Orbit B2 has 128 GB of internal storage. The Orbit B2 supports Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3 and 5G cellular.

## Software and warranty

The Orbit B2 ships with Lumen OS 4 and receives security updates for 6 years. The Orbit B2 comes with a 3 year warranty.
