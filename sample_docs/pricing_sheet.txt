LUMENFOLD CLOUD PRICING SHEET (2025)

Lumenfold Cloud is the inventory management software that pairs with Orbit scanners. This sheet covers software subscriptions only. Hardware is quoted separately by the sales team.

PLANS

Starter plan: 29 euros per user per month. The Starter plan supports up to 10 users and includes 5 GB of storage.

Team plan: 59 euros per user per month. The Team plan supports up to 100 users and includes 50 GB of storage. Single sign-on is not included in the Team plan.

Enterprise plan: 99 euros per user per month. The Enterprise plan has no user limit, includes single sign-on and a dedicated support manager, and carries a 99.95 percent uptime SLA.

DISCOUNTS

Customers who choose annual billing receive a 15 percent discount on any plan. Registered non-profit organisations receive a 30 percent discount. The two discounts cannot be combined.

TRIAL AND REFUNDS

Every plan starts with a free trial of 21 days. No payment card is needed to start the trial. Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, subscriptions are non-refundable.

SUPPORT

Starter plan support is by email with a response within 48 hours. Team plan support responds within 24 hours. Enterprise plan support responds within 1 hour, around the clock.

OVERAGES

Storage used beyond the plan allowance is billed at 0.40 euros per GB per month.
