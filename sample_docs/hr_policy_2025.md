# Lumenfold Systems Employee Handbook 2025

Effective from 1 January 2025. This edition replaces the 2024 handbook.

## Annual leave (2025)

In 2025, full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 8 unused days may be carried over into the following year, and carried-over days must be used by 30 April. Leave requests are submitted in the PeopleDesk portal at least 5 working days in advance.

## Sick leave (2025)

In 2025, employees receive 12 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required after 2 consecutive days of absence.

## Parental leave (2025)

Under the 2025 handbook, the primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of fully paid leave.

## Remote work (2025)

In 2025, employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time on all working days.

## Probation and notice (2025)

Under the 2025 handbook, the probation period for new hires is 3 months. During probation the notice period is 2 weeks for either side. After probation the notice period is 6 weeks.

## Learning budget (2025)

In 2025, each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 euros per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Overtime (2025)

In 2025, approved weekend overtime is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate. Weekday overtime is still compensated as time off in lieu.

## Sabbatical (new in 2025)

Employees who have completed 5 years of service may take an unpaid sabbatical of up to 4 weeks, subject to manager approval.
