# Lumenfold Systems Employee Handbook 2024

Effective from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2024. This edition is superseded by the 2025 handbook from 1 January 2025.

## Annual leave (2024)

In 2024, full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the following year, and carried-over days must be used by 31 March. Leave requests are submitted in the PeopleDesk portal at least 10 working days in advance.

## Sick leave (2024)

In 2024, employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive days of absence.

## Parental leave (2024)

Under the 2024 handbook, the primary caregiver receives 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 2 weeks of fully paid leave.

## Remote work (2024)

In 2024, employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time on all working days.

## Probation and notice (2024)

Under the 2024 handbook, the probation period for new hires is 6 months. During probation the notice period is 1 week for either side. After probation the notice period is 4 weeks.

## Learning budget (2024)

In 2024, each employee has a learning budget of 800 euros per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Overtime (2024)

In 2024, approved overtime is compensated as time off in lieu at a rate of one hour off for each hour worked. Overtime is not paid out in cash.
