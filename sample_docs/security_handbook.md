# Lumenfold Systems Security Handbook

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords must be rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts, with no exceptions.

## Reporting incidents

Suspected security incidents must be reported within 4 hours of discovery to security@lumenfold.example. A lost or stolen device must be reported within 1 hour so it can be remotely wiped.

## Devices

All company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may not be used to store company data.

## Guest Wi-Fi

Visitors use the network named Lumenfold-Guest, which is isolated from the corporate network. The guest network password rotates weekly and is handed out in person by reception. It is never written down in company documents.

## Data classification

Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data must not leave the EU region. Confidential data may be shared externally only under a signed non-disclosure agreement.

## Backups and training

Backups are taken daily and retained for 35 days. Every employee must complete security awareness training each year by 31 March.
