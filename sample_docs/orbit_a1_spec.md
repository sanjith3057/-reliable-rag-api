# Orbit A1 Handheld Scanner: Product Specification

The Orbit A1 is Lumenfold's entry-level handheld inventory scanner for retail stockrooms and small warehouses.

## Power

The Orbit A1 has a battery life of 14 hours of continuous scanning from its 4,200 mAh battery. A full charge of the Orbit A1 takes 2.5 hours over USB-C. The Orbit A1 battery is fixed and cannot be swapped in the field.

## Physical

The Orbit A1 weighs 310 grams. The Orbit A1 has an IP65 rating, which protects against dust and low-pressure water jets. Its display is a 4.7 inch touchscreen. The Orbit A1 operates between minus 10 and 50 degrees Celsius.

## Scanning

The Orbit A1 reads 1D and 2D barcodes at a range of up to 8 metres. It scans up to 60 codes per second.

## Storage and connectivity

The Orbit A1 has 64 GB of internal storage. The Orbit A1 supports Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.2. The Orbit A1 does not have a cellular modem.

## Software and warranty

The Orbit A1 ships with Lumen OS 3 and receives security updates for 4 years. The Orbit A1 comes with a 2 year warranty.
